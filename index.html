What day is Christmas?

Christmas Day is always on December 25, but the weekday moves forward by one day each year – and by two after a leap year. That is why Christmas 2027 lands on a Saturday and Christmas 2028 jumps to a Monday.

When Christmas falls on a weekend, many employers in the UK, Canada and elsewhere give a substitute day off on the following weekday.

FAQ

Christmas countdown questions

What day of the week is Christmas 2026?

Christmas Day 2026, December 25, falls on a Friday. Christmas Eve 2026 is on a Thursday.

How many sleeps until Christmas?

Sleeps are the number of nights left before Christmas morning. The countdown on this page shows the current number of sleeps, based on your device’s date.

When does the Christmas countdown reset?

The countdown shows a Christmas Day message on December 25 and starts counting down to the next Christmas from December 26.

Is the countdown based on my time zone?

Yes. It counts down to midnight on December 25 in the time zone of the device you are using, so it is correct wherever you are.

When should I start my Advent calendar?

Most Advent calendars start on December 1 and have 24 doors, one for each day until Christmas Eve.

Why is Christmas celebrated on December 25?

December 25 has been used by Western churches to mark the birth of Jesus since at least the 4th century. Many Orthodox churches that follow the Julian calendar celebrate on January 7.
